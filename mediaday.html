MORE THAN A TEAM PHOTO

A Media Day gives your athletes more than a traditional team picture: a collection of polished, high-energy images for social media, game-day graphics, senior recognition, posters, programs, and school athletics websites.

Every athlete gets direction on posing, expression, and equipment placement. We capture clean, traditional portraits while making room for personality, intensity, and creative shots that make the experience fun. The goal is a professional image library with a consistent look from the first athlete to the last.

BUILT AROUND YOUR SPORT

Lighting, poses, props, equipment, and the overall feel can be tailored to your team. 603 In Focus photographs Media Days for:

  • Basketball
  • Volleyball
  • Soccer
  • Lacrosse
  • Baseball and softball
  • Football
  • Hockey
  • Track and field
  • Other high school, club, and youth sports

Uniforms, balls, sticks, helmets, nets, and other equipment can all be part of the session. With multiple backdrops and color gels, we can also bring your school colors into the setup and create a custom look for your team. Before the shoot, we’ll talk through the look you want and how your program plans to use the images.

A SMOOTH DAY FOR COACHES & ADMINS

You need images that work for rosters, game programs, fundraising, and social—without adding chaos to practice or game week. We communicate timelines up front, keep the set organized, and coach posing so uniforms and equipment read clearly on camera.

WHAT YOUR TEAM CAN EXPECT

An organized, unhurried session planned with your coach, athletic director, or team contact—from roster and location to the final gallery.

Plan the session

Before Media Day, we coordinate the roster, location, available time, team-photo needs, and special requests with your coach, athletic director, or team contact.

Make the most of shoot day

Athletes rotate through the set with direction on posing. Depending on your package and available time, we can photograph individuals, action-inspired poses, small groups, captains, seniors, and the full team.

Edited images, ready to use

After the session, images are professionally edited and delivered digitally in a private online gallery. Your program can easily download and use them throughout the season, with files suitable for web, social, programs, and posters.
