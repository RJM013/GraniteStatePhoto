MEDIA DAY PHOTOGRAPHY CONSENT

Parent or guardian authorization for promotional use of Media Day photographs.

ABOUT THIS FORM

603 In Focus will photograph your athlete during their scheduled team Media Day. In addition to providing photographs to the team, selected photographs may occasionally be used by 603 In Focus to showcase our photography work on our website, Instagram, Facebook, portfolio, and printed marketing materials.

Granting promotional permission is completely optional. Choosing not to grant permission will not affect your athlete's participation in Media Day or the photographs created for the team.

If permission is granted, the athlete's name will not be used in promotional posts unless separately approved. Images will not be sold or licensed for unrelated third-party advertising or used in paid advertisements without additional permission.

Permission may be withdrawn in the future by contacting rob@603-in-focus.com. Upon receiving a withdrawal request, 603 In Focus will stop using the athlete's image in future promotional materials and will remove the image from our own website and social media accounts within a reasonable period.
