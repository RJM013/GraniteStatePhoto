Photographer Dad vs. Referee Dad: Shooting My Son's Championship Basketball Game

September 27, 2026

There are moments as a sports photographer when you have to make an important professional decision.

This past weekend, during the championship game between NSSA SC Varsity and Mass REVS at a sportsplex in Massachusetts, I had one of those moments.

The Problem With Being Dad AND the Photographer

Photographing your own kid's games is a little different than photographing a team you don't have an emotional connection to. When it's my son playing, Dad Rob and Photographer Rob have to coexist.

That balance matters. The best frames often happen when I stay locked in on the action, keep my mouth shut, and let the picture tell the story.
