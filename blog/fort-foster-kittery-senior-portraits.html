Was the Drive to Maine Worth It? A Senior Session at Fort Foster

August 30, 2026

There are certain photography decisions that sound questionable right up until you see the pictures.

Driving an hour to Maine for a senior session might qualify. But the combination of the pier, rocky shoreline, beach, open water, and older fort structures made the location feel like a full gallery in one place.

One Location, A Lot of Different Looks

One of the things I loved was how quickly the session could change without ever getting back into the truck. We had the pier, the beach, the rocks, the open water, and the concrete fort areas.

That variety gives a senior session a lot more life and helps the final gallery feel more complete.
