Media Day

Coe-Brown Girls Volleyball Media Day

September 14, 2026

There's something different about a great Media Day. It isn't just about lining athletes up in front of a backdrop and taking a few pictures. It's about creating an experience, building excitement for the season, and giving athletes images they're genuinely excited to share.

When I had the opportunity to photograph the Coe-Brown Northwood Academy Girls Varsity Volleyball team, I knew I wanted to create something that felt different from a traditional school picture day.

It Started in the Garage

I set up my gear in the garage and started testing different lighting arrangements. I wanted a red accent light to tie the images back to Coe-Brown's colors without overpowering the portraits.

By the time Media Day arrived, the setup was dialed in and the photos had the right balance of polish and energy.
