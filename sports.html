HOW IT WORKS

What you can expect when I cover your game, tournament, or athlete spotlight session.

Action shots

I position for key moments of play using high-speed capture so motion stays sharp—drives, saves, goals, and the plays that define the game.

Emotion & reactions

Beyond the play itself, I capture celebrations, focus between plays, and the camaraderie that makes sports meaningful for athletes and families.

Team & player moments

When schedules allow, I photograph team groups, individual athletes, and coach interactions—great for programs and personal keepsakes.

Tournaments & awards

For championship and tournament events, I stay through recognition moments so milestones are documented alongside the action.
